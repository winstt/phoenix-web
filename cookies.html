What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

How we use cookies

The Phoenix Community Trust website (phoenix-trust.co.uk) is a static informational site. We aim to keep our use of cookies to an absolute minimum. Currently, we use only the following:

Essential cookies

Third-party cookies
