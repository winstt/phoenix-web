Grants

Funding Justice in Action.

The Phoenix Community Trust is committed to redistributing power and resources to Global Majority communities across the UK — through grants, investment, and long-term community infrastructure.

By investing in Global Majority communities, we dismantle structural racism and strengthen the collective power of our communities.
