2026 Grants Round – What to Expect

May 12, 2026  ·  Phoenix Community Trust

The Phoenix Community Trust is preparing to launch its most significant funding opportunity to date — a £4 million grants round developed in partnership with the National Lottery Community Fund. This round is designed to redistribute meaningful resources directly to Global Majority-led organisations working at the heart of their communities across the UK. Applications will open in autumn 2026, with grants ranging from £50,000 to £500,000 available to eligible organisations.

Who can apply

Eligibility is focused on organisations that are led by and primarily serve Global Majority communities in the UK. We are particularly interested in groups operating at neighbourhood and regional level — those with deep roots and trusted relationships in their communities. Organisations do not need prior experience of large grant programmes; we are committed to providing accessible support throughout the application process, including one-to-one guidance sessions and plain-English guidance materials.

“This is not just a funding programme — it is a commitment to shifting power. We want resources to reach the people and places that have historically been overlooked by mainstream philanthropy.” — Phoenix Community Trust Director

How to register your interest

Organisations interested in applying are encouraged to register their interest now, ahead of the formal launch. Registering does not commit you to applying — it simply means you will receive programme updates, invitations to information webinars, and early access to draft guidance. You can register via the Grants section of our website or by emailing our grants team directly. We expect to host a series of regional information events throughout the summer to ensure organisations across all nine regions can access support in person.

The full timeline will be published in June 2026 alongside detailed eligibility criteria and an indicative application framework. We encourage all interested organisations to read the upcoming guidance carefully and to make use of the peer support networks Phoenix has established through its regional hubs. Updates will be shared via our newsletter and social channels — sign up via the Contact section of our website to stay informed.
