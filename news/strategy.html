2026 Strategy Development

April 28, 2026  ·  Phoenix Community Trust

As Phoenix enters its most active operational year to date, the organisation has begun a structured strategy development process to shape its direction through to 2028. This work is not being done behind closed doors — it is a genuinely participatory process, drawing on the insight and experience of our network of community organisations, regional leads, and the communities we ultimately exist to serve. The result will be a strategy that is owned as much by the movement as by the organisation.

Building foundations for systemic change

Our strategic priorities for 2026 centre on three interconnected pillars: power redistribution, infrastructure building, and narrative change. Power redistribution means moving beyond grant-making alone — it means ensuring that Global Majority communities have a genuine voice in how resources are allocated, how decisions are made, and who sits at tables that matter. Infrastructure building means strengthening the operational capacity of our network so that organisations are not just surviving but are positioned to lead and grow. Narrative change means challenging the dominant stories told about our communities and amplifying the expertise, creativity and leadership that have always existed within them.

“We are not building strategy for communities — we are building strategy with them. The expertise we need is already in the room.” — Phoenix Community Trust Chair

Community input process

Between May and July 2026, Phoenix will host a series of strategy listening sessions across its nine regions. These sessions are open to all organisations in our network, as well as community members who are not yet formally affiliated. We will be asking questions about what systemic change looks like in practice, what barriers communities are facing that Phoenix could help address, and what the Trust could do differently or better. The sessions will be informal and in-person where possible, with online options available for those who cannot attend in person.

A draft strategy document will be circulated for broader comment in September 2026, before a final version is published in November. We are committed to transparency throughout this process — working documents will be shared openly, and feedback will be acknowledged and responded to in writing. If you would like to participate in the consultation or host a listening session in your area, please get in touch through the Contact section of our website.
