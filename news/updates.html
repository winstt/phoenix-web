More Updates Coming Soon

January 1, 2026  ·  Phoenix Community Trust

We are in the process of building out our news archive and will be publishing regular updates throughout 2026 covering grants, events, partnerships, and stories from across our network. This is a big year for Phoenix — from the launch of our £4M grants round to regional convenings across all nine areas we work in — and we want to document and share that journey openly. Check back here regularly, as new articles will be added on an ongoing basis.

The best way to stay informed is to subscribe to our newsletter, which goes out monthly and includes the latest news, funding updates, and opportunities from across the Phoenix community. You can sign up via the Contact section of our website. We also share updates on social media — follow us to make sure you do not miss anything.
